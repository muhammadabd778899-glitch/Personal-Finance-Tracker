## Short description

A complete personal finance workspace you own. Track accounts, spending, budgets, bills, goals, debt and net worth in one calm app. No bank connection, no account, no subscription. Runs offline in your browser.

## Long description

**Your money, your device.**

Obsidian Finance is a one-time digital download for people who want a real budgeting app without handing bank credentials to a company or paying every month. It runs entirely in your browser and stores everything locally.

**What is included**

- Accounts: checking, savings, cash, investment, credit cards and loans
- Income, expense and transfer tracking with categories, payees and tags
- Monthly and custom budgets with category allocations
- Recurring bills and income with a calendar, paid/skipped tracking and rescheduling
- Savings goals and sinking funds
- Debt payoff estimates: snowball, avalanche and custom order
- Net worth history and a 45-day cash-flow forecast
- Reports with category breakdowns and CSV exports
- CSV import with duplicate detection and row preview
- Versioned JSON backup, validated restore and safe wipe
- Light and dark themes, installable offline app

**What makes it different**

- No bank connections, accounts, cloud sync or subscription
- Every number explains its period and formula
- Accounts and categories archive instead of losing history
- Backups are checksum- and relationship-validated before restore
- Accessible charts with data tables and keyboard-friendly dialogs

**How it works**

1. Download the ZIP.
2. Open the included `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
3. Load the sample workspace to explore, then create your own accounts.

Optional: host the included `dist` folder on any static host for a shareable URL.

**Important**

- This is a web app, not a native mobile app or spreadsheet.
- Data lives in your browser. Export backups regularly.
- No bank sync, no automatic imports, no financial advice.
- One workspace per browser origin. Move data with the JSON backup.
